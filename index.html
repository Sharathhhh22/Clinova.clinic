FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online through our website by clicking the 'Book Appointment' button or by calling our clinic directly. We will help you select a convenient time slot with your doctor.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Yes, we accept walk-in patients. However, booking an appointment in advance is highly recommended to secure your preferred doctor and minimize your waiting time.

What services does your clinic provide?

Our clinic provides general consultations, preventive care, cardiac care, diagnostic tests, vaccinations and continuous follow-up support tailored to your healthcare needs.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can easily reschedule or cancel your appointment. Please contact our support team or use the online portal at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled time.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes, we offer secure online video and phone consultations for many of our healthcare services, allowing you to connect with our specialists from home.
